About AUMly

AUMly brings timeless spiritual and philosophical texts directly to your mobile device with a distraction-free, privacy-first reading experience.

  • Core Collections: Gitas, 13 Principal Upanishads, Yoga & Darśana Sūtras, Shaiva & Tantras, Stotras & Hymns, and Vedānta.
  • Privacy First: Local storage strictly on your device. Your reading progression and preferences never sync to external developer servers.
